05 · Voice and writing

Speak like a good specialist

Clear, calm and specific. We explain what will happen, what it costs and who will do it. Plain British-leaning English, as used across Pakistan's private healthcare.

Explain, don't impress

Describe the treatment in the patient's words first, the clinical term second.

Advanced endodontic therapy with cutting-edge technology. Root canal treatment removes infection from inside the tooth so it can be kept.

Specific over superlative

Facts build trust; adjectives cost it. Every claim must be checkable.

World-class dentistry from Islamabad's top doctors. 14 dentists across two clinics, including an oral surgeon and a consultant orthodontist.

Name the cost

Give the price or a range, and say what can change it.

Affordable implants. Contact us for pricing. Implants from Rs 150,000. Your final cost is agreed after an examination.

Warm, never casual

Kind and human, without emoji, jokes or exclamation marks.

Get to know us better :) We love smiles! Meet the team who will look after you, and what they do outside the clinic.

06 · Imagery and icons

Real rooms, real people, daylight

Photography shows the actual clinics and the actual team. No stock smiles. Every example below is the clinic's own photography.

Portrait · Charcoal backdrop, black scrubs, arms relaxed. Same lens and height for every doctor.
Interior · Treatment room or lounge in natural light. Wide, calm, level horizon; nobody posing.
Detail · Instruments, materials, the etched door. Shallow depth, no blood.
Care · Hands, conversation, a child in the chair with a parent close.
Grade

Cool-neutral white balance, lifted shadows, gently reduced saturation. Greens in the room should read as the brand's evergreen, never neon.

Graphic device · the arch

Portraits and feature images may sit in an arch frame taken from the emblem. One arch per view; everything else uses a 20 px radius.

Avoid

Stock models, extreme close-ups of teeth, visible procedures, green colour overlays, text over busy photos without an Ink scrim of 55% or more.

IconsLine icons, 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps. Lucide as the source set. Evergreen in a Mist well.
