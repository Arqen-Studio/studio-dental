01 · Principles

Premium and clinical, still human

The website should feel state of the art without feeling cold. Each side of the brand has its own tools.

Clinical, state of the art

  • Cool Porcelain and Stone neutrals with generous space
  • Evergreen used sparingly, for action and identity
  • A strict type scale, tabular prices, hairline structure
  • Flat surfaces, three radii, quiet motion

Human and warm

  • Real photography of the team and the clinics in daylight
  • Champagne and Brass details: eyebrows, rules, highlights
  • Bricolage Grotesque's soft, characterful headlines
  • Plain, kind copy that explains before it sells

StudioDental.Logo

The Studio Dental wordmark with the arch emblem, rendered as live type so it stays sharp and themeable.

  • variant: horizontal (default, header and footer), stacked (square spaces, signage), emblem (favicon-sized spaces, avatars).
  • tone: brand on Porcelain/White, ink for one-colour print, inverse on Ink or Evergreen.
  • descriptor: "Clinic" by default; "Clinic · Islamabad" in the footer; null in the site header and below 140 px wide.
  • size is the emblem height in px. Keep clear space equal to the emblem's inner tooth on every side. Minimum 24 px.
  • Never recolour to mint, add effects, or re-type "Studio Dental" in another font.

09 · Accessibility

Built in, not added later

Interaction

  • Every tappable element is at least 44 × 44 px, including phone and map links
  • Visible 2 px focus ring, offset 2 px, on every interactive element
  • Motion 160–240 ms; respect prefers-reduced-motion
  • Content is visible at rest: no count-up from zero, no sections hidden until scrolled

Content

  • Text meets 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 at 24 px and up) in both themes
  • One h1 per page, headings in order
  • Form fields have visible labels; errors explain the fix
  • Meaningful images have alt text; decorative icons are hidden
